## Rules for forks and derivatives

You **may**:
- Fork the code and build your own product from it.
- Truthfully state that your product is *"based on Haang"* or *"derived from the Haang project"* for accurate attribution.

You **must not**, without written permission:
- Name your fork, product, or service "Haang" or "Little Tony", or use a confusingly similar name.
- Use the Haang / Little Tony logos or branding.
- Imply that your fork is the official Haang project, or that it is endorsed by or affiliated with the project.

If you distribute a modified version, please **rebrand it** with your own distinct name and logo.

## Why

Haang is a free tool for Cambodian SMEs, sustained by community support, donations, grants, and training. Keeping the name and logo reserved protects users from confusing an unofficial fork with the trusted, official project — while leaving the code fully open for anyone to build on.
